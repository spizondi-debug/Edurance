Careers with Maths Literacy

Maths Literacy opens many doors, but not all of them. Here's an honest guide.

Updated 26 September 2026

Maths Literacy is accepted for many degrees, diplomas and TVET qualifications. It is not accepted for fields built on mathematics, such as engineering, medicine, actuarial science, computer science and most BSc degrees.

Fields that need pure Mathematics

If you're interested in engineering, medicine, pharmacy, actuarial science, chartered accounting, computer science, architecture or most science degrees, you'll need Mathematics. If you're still in Grade 9 or early Grade 10, talk to your school about your options now.

Always check the latest rules. Each university sets its own subject minimums and APS, and they can change every year. Use this page as a guide, then confirm on the university's prospectus before you choose subjects or apply.

Frequently asked questions

Can I go to university with Maths Literacy?

Yes. Many degrees accept it, as long as you meet the pass type and APS requirements.

Can I switch from Maths Literacy to Maths?

Sometimes, early in Grade 10. Later, some learners upgrade their Maths after matric through an upgrade programme.

Is Maths Literacy a bad choice?

No. It suits many careers. Just make sure the careers you're interested in accept it.
