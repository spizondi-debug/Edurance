How this calculator works

Each subject mark is turned into an NSC achievement level from 1 to 7. Your APS is the total of your best six subjects. Life Orientation is left out, as most South African universities do. The highest possible score is 42.

Important: universities don't all count APS the same way

This calculator uses the standard scale. Some universities and faculties use their own version: they may add points for high marks, count Life Orientation, or use percentages instead. Your APS is also only one requirement. Most courses also set minimum marks for specific subjects, such as Mathematics or English.

Always check the admission rules on the university's own website before you apply. In the EduRance app, you can see which courses match your APS and your subject marks.

Which marks should I use?

In Grade 12, universities usually make provisional offers based on your Grade 11 final results or your Grade 12 June results. Your final place depends on your NSC results. In Grade 10 or 11, use your latest report to see where you stand and which subjects to work on.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good APS score?

It depends on what you want to study. Roughly, a score in the low 20s opens many higher certificates and diplomas, the high 20s to low 30s open many degrees, and competitive degrees such as medicine or engineering usually need the mid-30s or more. Always check the specific course.

Is Life Orientation included in APS?

Most universities leave it out, and this calculator does too. A few institutions give it a small value, so check the university's own rules.

What is the maximum APS?

Using the standard method, 42: six subjects × 7 points.

Can I use Grade 11 marks?

Yes. Most universities make provisional offers on your Grade 11 final or Grade 12 June results, then confirm with your final NSC results.
