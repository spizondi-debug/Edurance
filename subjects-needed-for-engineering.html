Subjects needed for engineering

Engineering has the strictest subject rules of any field. Choose correctly in Grade 9.

Updated 26 September 2026

You can study a four-year engineering degree (BEng or BSc Eng) at a university, or a three-year engineering diploma at a university of technology. Degrees accredited by the Engineering Council of South Africa (ECSA) lead to registration as a professional engineer.

School subjects you'll usually need

SubjectWhy it matters
MathematicsRequired, usually with a high mark. Mathematical Literacy is not accepted for engineering degrees.
Physical SciencesRequired for almost every engineering programme.
EnglishNeeded for reports and communication; most programmes set a minimum.
Technical Mathematics and Technical SciencesAccepted for some engineering diplomas at universities of technology, but usually not for degrees.

Degree or diploma?

Degrees are more theoretical and lead to professional engineer registration. Diplomas are more hands-on and lead to engineering technician roles, with the option to continue studying later.

Always check the latest rules. Each university sets its own subject minimums and APS, and they can change every year. Use this page as a guide, then confirm on the university's prospectus before you choose subjects or apply.

Three checks before applying for engineering

  1. Confirm that the programme accepts your Mathematics or Technical Mathematics subject and mark.
  2. Check the Physical Sciences requirement and the institution's own APS or selection score.
  3. Compare university engineering degrees with university of technology diplomas; they lead to different study routes and may use different entry rules.

Meeting a minimum does not guarantee selection where places are limited. Compare several accredited programmes and read each institution's current prospectus before applying.

Frequently asked questions

Can I study engineering with Maths Literacy?

No. Engineering degrees and diplomas need Mathematics, or Technical Mathematics for some diplomas.

Do I need Physical Sciences for engineering?

Yes, for almost every programme.

Are there bursaries for engineering?

Yes. Engineering is one of the best-funded fields, with many company bursaries. See our engineering bursaries guide.
