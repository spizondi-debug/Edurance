Subjects needed for teaching

The subjects you choose now affect which subjects and grades you can teach later.

Updated 26 September 2026

To teach in South Africa you need a four-year Bachelor of Education (BEd), or a degree followed by a one-year Postgraduate Certificate in Education (PGCE). You'll train for a phase: Foundation (Grade R–3), Intermediate (Grade 4–6), Senior (Grade 7–9) or FET (Grade 10–12).

School subjects you'll usually need

SubjectWhy it matters
EnglishNeeded for every teaching qualification. You'll teach and assess in the language of instruction.
The subject you want to teachTo teach a subject in high school, you usually need it at school. For example, to teach Mathematics you'll need Mathematics, not Maths Literacy.
Mathematics or Mathematical LiteracyFoundation and Intermediate Phase programmes often accept Maths Literacy. Maths teaching needs Mathematics.
An African languageVery useful, especially for Foundation Phase, where learners are often taught in their home language.

Funding your teaching degree

The Department of Basic Education's Funza Lushaka bursary funds students training to teach in priority areas. In return you teach at a public school for the same number of years you were funded. See our teaching bursaries guide.

Always check the latest rules. Each university sets its own subject minimums and APS, and they can change every year. Use this page as a guide, then confirm on the university's prospectus before you choose subjects or apply.

Match your subjects to the phase you want to teach

Foundation Phase, Intermediate Phase and Senior Phase/FET teaching programmes have different entry rules. If you want to teach a secondary-school subject, universities usually expect a suitable school subject and later university modules in that specialisation.

  1. Choose the age group or phase you want to teach.
  2. Check the university's language and subject specialisation requirements.
  3. Compare a BEd with a suitable bachelor's degree followed by an approved teaching qualification.

Frequently asked questions

Can I study teaching with Maths Literacy?

Yes, for many Foundation and Intermediate Phase programmes. To teach Mathematics you'll need Mathematics.

Which teaching subjects are in demand?

Mathematics, Physical Sciences, technical subjects and African languages are consistently in demand in South African schools.

How long is a teaching degree?

A BEd takes four years. A PGCE takes one year after another degree.
