UNISA applications 2027: closing date and how to apply

Study from anywhere with Africa’s largest distance-learning university. Here’s how to apply for 2027 before 9 October.

Updated 25 September 2026

Applications opened17 August 2026
Closing date9 October 2026
Study modeDistance learning

These dates are for UNISA's undergraduate qualifications, honours degrees and postgraduate diplomas for 2027. UNISA doesn't give extensions, so treat 9 October as a hard deadline.

Why choose UNISA?

UNISA is Africa's largest distance-learning university. You study from home on your own schedule, which suits learners who need to work, live far from a campus or want to keep costs down. It offers higher certificates, diplomas and degrees.

Admission requirements

Every UNISA qualification lists its own requirements. As a rule, a higher certificate needs a Higher Certificate pass, a diploma needs a Diploma pass, and a degree needs a Bachelor's pass, plus any subject requirements for that qualification. If you don't qualify for a degree yet, a UNISA higher certificate can be a route into one later.

How to apply to UNISA

  1. Choose your qualification on unisa.ac.za and check its admission requirements.
  2. Apply online during the application period and pay the application fee shown on the website.
  3. Upload certified copies of your ID and your results. UNISA asks for certifications that are no older than six months.
  4. Track your application and respond quickly to any offer.

Funding

NSFAS can fund qualifying UNISA students. NSFAS 2027 applications close on 18 November 2026.

Frequently asked questions

When do UNISA 2027 applications close?

9 October 2026 for undergraduate qualifications, honours degrees and postgraduate diplomas.

Can I study at UNISA straight after matric?

Yes, if you meet the admission requirements for the qualification you choose.

Does NSFAS fund UNISA?

Yes, qualifying students at UNISA can be funded by NSFAS.
