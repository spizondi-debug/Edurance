What is EduRance?

One app for learning, progress and opportunity.

EduRance brings a learner’s school journey into one place: marks and APS, subject practice, past papers and mock exams, career discovery, and matched courses, bursaries and learnerships. Parents can follow progress through a secure read-only link, so everyone can see what the learner needs next.

How EduRance works

EduRance guides the learner through a connected five-step journey. Every activity, result and achievement helps make the next step clearer.

01

Create a learner profile

Add your grade, school, subjects, marks and goals.

02

Follow a study plan

Practise with past papers, mock exams, video lessons and AI tutors, guided by your Exam Coach plan.

03

Measure real progress

See your marks, APS history, assessment results, streaks, XP and badges in one place.

04

Get focused support

AI Tutor, Voice Tutor and AI Career Coach help any time, and parents can follow progress through a secure read-only link.

05

Move to the next opportunity

Match your marks to courses, and discover bursaries, learnerships, internships and career pathways.
