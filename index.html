What is EduRance?

One app for learning, progress and opportunity.

EduRance brings the important parts of a learner’s journey into one connected place. Learners can access courses, complete activities and assessments, set personal goals, see their achievements and discover opportunities that match their progress. Parents, tutors, educators and institutions can provide better support because everyone has a clearer view of what the learner needs next.

A journey with direction

Complete EduRance Learner Journey

  1. 01

    Create your learner profile

    Add your grade, subjects, marks, interests and future plans.

  2. 02

    Calculate your APS score

    Enter your Grade 11 or Matric results, and EduRance calculates your Admission Point Score automatically.

  3. 03

    See what you qualify for

    Discover university courses, TVET programmes, learnerships and skills programmes matching your subjects, marks and APS.

  4. 04

    Complete the Interest Assessment

    Answer questions about your interests, personality, strengths and preferred working environment.

  5. 05

    Complete a Learning Assessment

    Take short subject assessments to identify what you understand and where you are struggling.

  6. 06

    Receive your personalised results

    View your strengths, learning gaps and subjects or topics requiring additional support.

  7. 07

    Get a personalised improvement plan

    Receive recommended lessons, practice activities, study resources and tutor support based on your assessment results.

  8. 08

    Discover suitable careers

    Explore careers matching your interests, strengths, personality, subjects and academic performance.

  9. 09

    Explore each career in detail

    Learn about:

    • What the career involves
    • Required school subjects
    • Minimum APS and admission requirements
    • Qualifications and institutions
    • Study duration
    • Estimated salaries
    • Employment prospects
    • Related careers
  10. 10

    Save your favourite careers

    Build a personalised shortlist of careers, qualifications, institutions and opportunities.

  11. 11

    Set your academic and career goals

    Create goals for improving marks, completing assessments, applying to institutions and preparing for your chosen career.

  12. 12

    Follow your personalised journey

    EduRance creates a step-by-step pathway showing what you must learn, improve and complete to reach your goals.

  13. 13

    Track your progress

    Use your dashboard to monitor assessments, marks, learning activities, saved careers, goals and applications.

  14. 14

    Earn badges and celebrate achievements

    Unlock badges for:

    • Completing your profile
    • Calculating your APS
    • Finishing assessments
    • Improving subject results
    • Maintaining learning streaks
    • Reaching study goals
    • Exploring careers
    • Completing applications
  15. 15

    Receive helpful reminders

    Get notifications for upcoming assessments, study sessions, unfinished goals, application deadlines, bursary deadlines and scheduled tutor sessions.

  16. 16

    Access personalised support

    Connect with learning resources, tutors, educators and career guidance when you need assistance.

  17. 17

    Find and apply for opportunities

    Discover universities, colleges, bursaries, learnerships, internships and other opportunities matching your profile.

  18. 18

    Update and improve your journey

    Add new marks and assessment results so EduRance can update your recommendations, goals and career pathway.
