What is EduRance?

One app for learning, progress and opportunity.

EduRance brings a learner’s school journey into one place: marks and APS, subject practice, past papers and mock exams, career discovery, and matched courses, bursaries and learnerships. Parents can follow progress through a secure read-only link, so everyone can see what the learner needs next.

A journey with direction

Complete EduRance Learner Journey

  1. 01

    Create your learner profile

    Add your grade, subjects, marks, interests and future plans.

  2. 02

    Calculate your APS score

    From Grade 10, enter your subject marks and EduRance calculates your Admission Point Score using the official rules: your best six subjects, excluding Life Orientation. Grade 8–9 learners get subject-choice guidance instead.

  3. 03

    See what you qualify for

    Discover university and college courses, learnerships and skills programmes matching your subjects, marks and APS. Use the simulator to see how a better mark changes your options.

  4. 04

    Complete the Career Discovery Assessment

    Answer 30 questions about your interests and strengths. Your answers save automatically, so you can finish later.

  5. 05

    Take practice assessments

    Complete auto-marked subject assessments to see what you understand and where you are struggling.

  6. 06

    Receive your personalised results

    View your strengths, learning gaps and subjects or topics requiring additional support.

  7. 07

    Get a personalised improvement plan

    Receive recommended practice questions, video lessons, past papers and AI Tutor help based on your assessment results.

  8. 08

    Discover suitable careers

    Get recommended careers with match percentages and a personal explanation of why each one suits you. Grade 8–9 learners also get Grade 10 subject recommendations. Try a day in the life with the Career Simulator.

  9. 09

    Explore each career in detail

    Learn about:

    • What the career involves
    • Required school subjects
    • Minimum APS and admission requirements
    • Qualifications and institutions
    • Study duration
    • Estimated salaries
    • Employment prospects
    • Related careers
  10. 10

    Save your favourite careers

    Build a personalised shortlist of careers, qualifications, institutions and opportunities.

  11. 11

    Set your academic and career goals

    Create goals for improving marks, completing assessments, applying to institutions and preparing for your chosen career.

  12. 12

    Follow your personalised journey

    Exam Coach turns your target marks into a study plan with a countdown to your exams, and your roadmap tracks your milestones along the way.

  13. 13

    Track your progress

    Use your dashboard to monitor assessments, marks, learning activities, saved careers, goals and applications.

  14. 14

    Earn badges and celebrate achievements

    Unlock badges for:

    • Completing your profile
    • Calculating your APS
    • Finishing assessments
    • Improving subject results
    • Maintaining learning streaks
    • Reaching study goals
    • Exploring careers
    • Watching video lessons
  15. 15

    Receive helpful reminders

    Get reminders for study sessions, unfinished goals, application deadlines and bursary deadlines, in the app, by push notification and by email.

  16. 16

    Get help when you need it

    Ask the AI Tutor, Voice Tutor or AI Career Coach, join the moderated learner community, and share your progress with a parent through a secure link.

  17. 17

    Find and apply for opportunities

    Discover universities, colleges, bursaries, learnerships and internships matching your profile, with deadlines and eligibility details. Use Work Readiness to build your CV, practise interviews and write application emails.

  18. 18

    Update and improve your journey

    Add new term marks and assessment results so EduRance can update your recommendations, goals and career pathway. You can retake the Career Discovery Assessment after six months.
