What is EduRance?

One app for learning, progress and opportunity.

EduRance brings the important parts of a learner’s journey into one connected place. Learners can access courses, complete activities and assessments, set personal goals, see their achievements and discover opportunities that match their progress. Parents, tutors, educators and institutions can provide better support because everyone has a clearer view of what the learner needs next.

How EduRance works

EduRance guides the learner through a connected five-step journey. Every activity, result and achievement helps make the next step clearer.

  1. 1

    Create a learner profile

    Add education history, interests, goals and areas where support is needed.

  2. 2

    Follow a learning pathway

    Work through structured courses, lessons, activities and assessments connected to personal goals.

  3. 3

    Measure real progress

    See completed work, assessment results, learning streaks, milestones and certificates in one place.

  4. 4

    Receive focused support

    Parents, tutors and educators can use progress information to provide help where it matters most.

  5. 5

    Move to the next opportunity

    Discover relevant bursaries, learnerships, internships, further study and career pathways.

Designed around people

One platform. Many learning journeys.

EduRance gives every person in the learning ecosystem a clearer way to participate, support progress and make confident decisions.

L

Learners

Access courses and assessments, set goals, build learning streaks, earn achievements and discover the next suitable opportunity.

P

Parents

See milestones, upcoming work and areas where support is needed, making it easier to encourage meaningful progress at home.

E

Educators

Organise learning, review assessment performance and identify which learners or topics require additional attention.

T

Tutors

Use learner goals and performance information to prepare focused sessions and measure improvement over time.

S

Schools & training providers

Manage learning pathways, engagement, assessments and learner progress from one connected institutional view.

I

Colleges & universities

Support students with structured resources, progress insight and clearer pathways into further study, work and development.
