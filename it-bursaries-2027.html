IT bursaries 2027 in South Africa

Where to find funding for IT, computer science and information systems.

Updated 26 September 2026

Banks, telecoms companies, tech firms and retailers all need IT skills, and many fund students studying IT, computer science or information systems.

Where IT funding comes from

  • Company bursaries from banks, insurers, telecoms and tech companies, often with a work-back agreement.
  • The MICT SETA, the sector training authority for media, information and communication technologies, funds students in ICT skills.
  • Universities offer their own bursaries for IT and computer science students.
  • NSFAS funds qualifying students at public universities and TVET colleges. See NSFAS 2027.

Check your subjects

Computer science degrees require Mathematics. Some IT diplomas accept Maths Literacy. See subjects needed for IT.

When to apply

Start looking in Grade 11 and apply in Grade 12. Many bursaries close before matric results come out, and deadlines change every year, so check each sponsor's website and set reminders. The EduRance app lists verified bursaries and reminds you before they close.

Stay safe

Real bursaries never charge an application fee. Apply on the sponsor's own website, not through WhatsApp links or agents, and never share your banking PIN or one-time PIN.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get an IT bursary with Maths Literacy?

Some sponsors fund IT diplomas that accept Maths Literacy, but most degree bursaries need Mathematics.

Do IT bursaries cover laptops?

Some do. Check what each bursary covers before you accept it.

Is IT at school required?

No. Most sponsors care more about your Mathematics and English marks.
