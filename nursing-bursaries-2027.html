Nursing bursaries 2027 in South Africa

Where nursing funding comes from, and what the conditions usually are.

Updated 26 September 2026

Nursing is a scarce skill in South Africa, and several kinds of sponsors fund nursing students. Many expect you to work for them after you qualify.

Where nursing funding comes from

  • Provincial departments of health fund nursing students, often at public nursing colleges, usually with a work-back agreement at a public facility.
  • Private hospital groups run their own nursing training and sometimes fund students in return for work after qualifying.
  • NSFAS funds qualifying students at public universities and colleges. See NSFAS 2027.
  • Universities offer their own bursaries for health science students.

Check your subjects first

Most nursing sponsors want learners who meet the entry requirements. See subjects needed for nursing.

When to apply

Start looking in Grade 11 and apply in Grade 12. Many bursaries close before matric results come out, and deadlines change every year, so check each sponsor's website and set reminders. The EduRance app lists verified bursaries and reminds you before they close.

Stay safe

Real bursaries never charge an application fee. Apply on the sponsor's own website, not through WhatsApp links or agents, and never share your banking PIN or one-time PIN.

Frequently asked questions

Do nursing bursaries have to be paid back?

Usually not in cash, but most require you to work for the sponsor for a set time after you qualify.

Can I get a nursing bursary with Maths Literacy?

Often yes, if the institution you apply to accepts Maths Literacy for nursing.

Is nursing covered by NSFAS?

Yes, for qualifying students at public universities and TVET colleges.
