What can I study with my APS?

A practical guide to your options at every APS range, from higher certificates and TVET to competitive degrees.

Updated 25 September 2026

Your APS tells you which level of study you can aim for. Your NSC pass type sets the minimum, and each course adds its own APS and subject requirements. Here's how it fits together.

Step 1: Your NSC pass type

Pass typeWhat you needOpens the door to

Step 2: Rough guide by APS

These ranges are a general guide only. Requirements differ between universities and courses, so always check the prospectus.

APS below 20

Look at higher certificates (at universities, UNISA and private colleges), TVET college programmes such as NC(V) and N-courses, and learnerships and skills programmes. A higher certificate can lead on to a diploma later.

APS 20 to 24

Many diplomas at universities of technology and comprehensive universities, extended programmes (degrees or diplomas spread over an extra year with more support), and higher certificates.

APS 25 to 29

A wider range of diplomas and some degrees, for example in education, humanities, social sciences and some commerce programmes, depending on the university and your subject marks.

APS 30 to 34

Most degrees at many universities, including many commerce, science and law programmes, as long as you also meet the subject requirements, often Mathematics.

APS 35 and above

Competitive degrees such as medicine, engineering, actuarial science, and accounting (CA stream) at top universities. These often also need high Mathematics and Physical Sciences marks, and sometimes the NBT.

Subjects matter as much as APS

  • Mathematics vs Mathematical Literacy. Engineering, most sciences, health sciences and many commerce degrees require pure Mathematics.
  • Physical Sciences is often required for engineering, medicine and some science degrees.
  • English minimums apply to many courses, and law and teaching often set higher language marks.

Don't qualify yet? You still have options

  • Start with a higher certificate or TVET programme, then move up.
  • Choose an extended programme, which has lower entry marks and more support.
  • Upgrade your marks by rewriting subjects through the Second Chance Matric Programme.
  • Apply for a learnership. You earn a stipend and get a qualification while you work.

Frequently asked questions

What can I study with an APS of 20?

Mostly higher certificates, some diplomas and extended programmes, TVET college programmes and learnerships. Check each course's subject requirements.

What can I study with an APS of 25?

Many diplomas and some degrees, often in education, humanities or social sciences, depending on the university and your subject marks.

What can I study with an APS of 30?

Most degrees at many universities, as long as you meet the subject requirements, such as a minimum Mathematics mark.

Is Mathematical Literacy accepted for degrees?

For some degrees, yes, for example many humanities, education and social science programmes. Engineering, most sciences and health sciences require pure Mathematics.
