UJ applications 2027: closing date, APS and how to apply

Everything you need to apply to the University of Johannesburg for 2027 before the 31 October deadline.

Updated 25 September 2026

Applications opened1 April 2026
Closing date31 Oct 2026, 12:00
Online applicationFree

UJ has the latest closing date of any major South African university, and applying online is free. That makes it a smart backup even if UJ isn't your first choice. UJ does not accept late applications, and the deadline is at midday, not midnight.

How UJ calculates your APS

UJ uses your best six subjects, excluding Life Orientation, on the standard 1–7 scale, so the maximum is 42. Each programme sets its own minimum APS and subject requirements in the UJ 2027 undergraduate prospectus. Meeting the minimum doesn't guarantee a place, because selection is competitive. Work out your score with the free APS calculator.

How to apply to UJ

  1. Choose up to two programmes from the UJ 2027 prospectus. You can't change them after you submit.
  2. Apply online on the official UJ website (uj.ac.za). Answer "No" if you don't have a student number yet, and write down the PIN you create.
  3. Upload your ID and your Grade 11 final results (or matric results if you've finished school).
  4. If you need a room, apply for residence by the same 31 October deadline. Admission doesn't guarantee accommodation.
  5. Track your status on the UJ student portal.

UJ gives a provisional answer based on your Grade 11 results and confirms it with your final NSC results. UJ warns that anyone charging you to apply online is running a scam.

Funding your studies at UJ

UJ is a public university, so NSFAS can fund qualifying students. NSFAS 2027 applications close on the same day, 31 October 2026. Also look at other bursaries.

Frequently asked questions

Is the UJ application free?

Yes, applying online through UJ's official website is free.

When does UJ close for 2027?

31 October 2026 at 12:00 midday. UJ does not accept late applications.

How many courses can I apply for at UJ?

Two programmes.

Can I apply with Grade 11 results?

Yes. UJ makes a provisional decision on your Grade 11 final results, then confirms it against your final NSC results.
