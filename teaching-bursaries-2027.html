Teaching bursaries 2027 in South Africa

How to fund a teaching degree, starting with the country's biggest teaching bursary.

Updated 26 September 2026

South Africa needs more teachers, especially in Mathematics, Physical Sciences, technical subjects and African languages, so there is dedicated funding for teaching students.

Get your university offer first. Funza Lushaka only accepts applications once you have a student number and have been accepted or provisionally accepted, so apply to universities before the end of September.

Funza Lushaka bursary

Run by the Department of Basic Education, Funza Lushaka funds students studying a BEd or PGCE in priority teaching areas. It can cover tuition, accommodation, meals and learning materials. In return, you teach at a public school for the same number of years you received the bursary. Apply only on the official Funza Lushaka website.

Other funding for teaching

  • NSFAS funds qualifying students at public universities, including BEd students. See NSFAS 2027.
  • Provincial education departments sometimes fund local students in scarce subjects.
  • Universities offer their own bursaries and merit awards for education students.

Choose the right subjects

To teach a subject in high school, you usually need it at school. See subjects needed for teaching.

When to apply

Start looking in Grade 11 and apply in Grade 12. Many bursaries close before matric results come out, and deadlines change every year, so check each sponsor's website and set reminders. The EduRance app lists verified bursaries and reminds you before they close.

Stay safe

Real bursaries never charge an application fee. Apply on the sponsor's own website, not through WhatsApp links or agents, and never share your banking PIN or one-time PIN.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to pay back Funza Lushaka?

Not if you meet the conditions. You repay it by teaching at a public school for the same number of years you were funded.

Can I get Funza Lushaka and NSFAS?

Usually not for the same costs. Funza Lushaka is typically the better fit for teaching students who qualify.

Which subjects does Funza Lushaka prioritise?

Priority areas are set by the Department of Basic Education and can change. Check the official website for the current list.
