A simple way to choose Grade 10 subjects

  1. Write down three careers or study fields you would seriously consider.
  2. Check the compulsory subjects and minimum marks for several programmes in each field.
  3. Choose the combination that keeps those routes open and still suits your strengths.
  4. Review your choice with a teacher, parent or career adviser before the school deadline.

The Department of Basic Education confirms that learners choose three optional subjects alongside two languages, Mathematics or Mathematical Literacy, and Life Orientation. Its subject choice and career pathing guide is a useful official starting point.

Do not choose a subject only because friends are taking it. Start with the study routes you may want, confirm their subject requirements, and choose subjects you can work at consistently.

Frequently asked questions

When do I choose my subjects?

Most South African learners choose their FET subjects at the end of Grade 9, for Grade 10 to 12.

Should I take Maths or Maths Literacy?

If there's any chance you'll want engineering, medicine, science, IT or chartered accounting, choose Mathematics. Many other careers accept Maths Literacy.

Can I change subjects later?

Sometimes, early in Grade 10. After that it gets harder, so choose carefully.
