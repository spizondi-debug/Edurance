Subjects needed for psychology

The subjects to choose for a psychology degree, and the full route to becoming a psychologist.

Updated 26 September 2026

You can study psychology as part of a BA, a BSocSci or a BSc. To practise as a psychologist in South Africa you must register with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA), which takes further study after your first degree.

School subjects you'll usually need

SubjectWhy it matters
EnglishPsychology involves a lot of reading and writing, so universities look for a good English mark.
Mathematics or Mathematical LiteracyA BA or BSocSci often accepts either. A BSc in psychology usually needs Mathematics. Statistics is part of every psychology degree.
Life SciencesNot always required, but useful for understanding the brain and behaviour.

The route to becoming a psychologist

First complete a three-year degree majoring in psychology, then an Honours year. To become a clinical, counselling or educational psychologist you then need a Master's degree, an internship and community service before registering with the HPCSA. The BPsych is a separate four-year degree that leads to registration as a registered counsellor.

Always check the latest rules. Each university sets its own subject minimums and APS, and they can change every year. Use this page as a guide, then confirm on the university's prospectus before you choose subjects or apply.

Understand the full psychology study route

A school subject does not qualify someone as a psychologist. The route normally begins with an undergraduate degree that includes psychology, followed by further selection and postgraduate professional training for registered practice.

  1. Check the undergraduate degree's language, APS and Mathematics or Mathematical Literacy rules.
  2. Compare BA and BSc routes because their supporting subjects differ.
  3. Research the postgraduate route early; entry into an undergraduate psychology major does not guarantee professional registration later.

Frequently asked questions

Can I study psychology with Maths Literacy?

Often yes, for a BA or BSocSci. A BSc in psychology usually needs Mathematics.

How long does it take to become a psychologist?

Usually six to eight years: a degree, Honours, a Master's and an internship, plus community service.

What can I do with a psychology degree?

Many graduates work in human resources, social research, community work or education, or continue studying to register as a psychologist.
