Careers with Geography

Geography leads to careers in planning, the environment, mapping and more.

Updated 26 September 2026

Geography teaches you how people, places and the environment connect. Some careers below also need Mathematics, so check the subject column before you choose.

Careers that use Geography

Town and regional plannerMathematics is often required.

Study routes that build on Geography

Geography connects to environmental management, geographic information systems, urban and regional planning, climate and earth sciences, conservation, teaching, tourism and development studies.

Before you choose a course

  1. Check whether the route needs Mathematics, Physical Sciences or Life Sciences alongside Geography.
  2. Read the module list to see how much fieldwork, mapping, data analysis or policy work is included.
  3. Compare science, humanities and technology programmes because they prepare learners for different kinds of work.

Frequently asked questions

Is Geography a good subject for Grade 10?

Yes. It pairs well with both Maths and Maths Literacy, and it's useful for planning, environmental and tourism careers.

Can I do town planning with Maths Literacy?

Usually not at university level. Most planning degrees need Mathematics.

What pays well with Geography?

Technical fields such as GIS, surveying and planning tend to pay well, but they usually need Mathematics.
