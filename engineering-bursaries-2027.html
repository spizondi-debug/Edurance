Engineering bursaries 2027 in South Africa

Engineering is one of the best-funded fields in South Africa. Here's where to look.

Updated 26 September 2026

Companies in mining, energy, construction, manufacturing and infrastructure hire engineers, so many fund engineering students, usually with a work-back agreement.

In Grade 11? Most big engineering bursaries close during your matric year, some as early as May. Get your Grade 11 marks up, then apply as soon as windows open. Sasol, for example, opened in April for the 2027 intake.

Where engineering funding comes from

  • Company bursaries from large employers in mining, energy, construction, manufacturing and water.
  • SETA bursaries from sector training authorities that fund scarce engineering skills.
  • Universities offer engineering bursaries and merit awards.
  • NSFAS funds qualifying students at public universities. See NSFAS 2027.

What sponsors look for

Engineering sponsors usually want strong Mathematics and Physical Sciences marks, and many look at your Grade 11 results. See subjects needed for engineering.

When to apply

Start looking in Grade 11 and apply in Grade 12. Many bursaries close before matric results come out, and deadlines change every year, so check each sponsor's website and set reminders. The EduRance app lists verified bursaries and reminds you before they close.

Stay safe

Real bursaries never charge an application fee. Apply on the sponsor's own website, not through WhatsApp links or agents, and never share your banking PIN or one-time PIN.

Frequently asked questions

What marks do I need for an engineering bursary?

Most sponsors want strong Mathematics and Physical Sciences marks. Requirements differ by sponsor.

Do I have to work for the company after?

Usually yes, for about the same number of years you were funded.

Can I apply for more than one bursary?

Yes. Apply to several, but accept only one for the same costs.
