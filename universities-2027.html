Missed your first choice? What to do now

  • Apply to UJ before 31 October 2026. The online application is free and it closes later than any other major university.
  • Apply to UNISA before 9 October 2026 if distance learning suits you.
  • Apply for NSFAS before 31 October 2026. It's separate from your university application. See the NSFAS 2027 guide.
  • Watch for late and space-available applications in January, after matric results. Some universities and TVET colleges open these briefly.

Every university counts APS a little differently

Most universities add up your best six subjects, excluding Life Orientation. Some use their own scales instead: Wits, UCT (which uses a Faculty Points Score) and Nelson Mandela University (which uses its own Applicant Score) are examples. Check the prospectus of each university, and use the free APS calculator as a starting point.

Frequently asked questions

Which universities are still open for 2027 applications?

As of 25 September 2026: UJ (until 31 October), UNISA (until 9 October) and Sol Plaatje University (general applications until 31 October). Several others close on 30 September 2026.

Which universities have free applications?

UJ (online), UFS, NWU, Sol Plaatje University and Nelson Mandela University (for SA applicants) have free applications for 2027.

Can I apply to more than one university?

Yes, and you should. Apply to a mix of first-choice and backup options, and check each one's closing date.
